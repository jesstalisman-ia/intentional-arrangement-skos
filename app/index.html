Align · map · federate

Crosswalk taxonomies into a thesaurus

Pick a second taxonomy (another project in your workspace) and align its concepts with this one using the five SKOS mapping relations — exactMatch, closeMatch, broadMatch, narrowMatch, relatedMatch. Auto-match seeds the alignment from matching labels; you confirm and refine. Export the crosswalk on its own, or the whole federated thesaurus (both schemes + the mappings) as one interoperable RDF file.

The natural-language box maps common questions to SPARQL you can edit. Supported: SELECT/DISTINCT, BGP, a, prefixes & IRIs, OPTIONAL, FILTER, single-predicate paths p+/p*, COUNT/SUM with GROUP BY, ORDER BY, LIMIT. Prefix : is your base namespace. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

  






  

    
Import taxonomy 

    

      
Choose a file or paste below, then Import. Loads a SKOS taxonomy from RDF (Turtle, RDF/XML, JSON-LD, RDF/JSON) or a spreadsheet (CSV or Excel .xlsx). SKOS-XL labels resolve to plain labels.

      
      
Building in a spreadsheet? Download the CSV template — one row per concept; the broader column names the parent.

      

        
        
        
        Applies to CSV/Excel labels with no tag. BCP 47 / ISO 639 (e.g. nl, nl-BE). Tag any single cell as term@nl, or add a lang column per row.

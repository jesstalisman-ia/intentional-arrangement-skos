The natural-language box maps common questions to SPARQL you can edit. Supported: SELECT/DISTINCT, BGP, a, prefixes & IRIs, OPTIONAL, FILTER, single-predicate paths p+/p*, COUNT/SUM with GROUP BY, ORDER BY, LIMIT. Prefix : is your base namespace. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

  






  

    
Import taxonomy 

    

      
Choose a file or paste below, then Import. Loads a SKOS taxonomy from RDF (Turtle, RDF/XML, JSON-LD, RDF/JSON) or a spreadsheet (CSV or Excel .xlsx). SKOS-XL labels resolve to plain labels.

      
      
Building in a spreadsheet? Download the CSV template — one row per concept; the broader column names the parent.
